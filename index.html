Our Practice

Small on purpose. Local by choice.

Crete Dental Group is an independent dental office on E Steger Road — the kind of place where the front desk learns your name, your kids' names, and probably which school they go to. We're not part of a corporate group, which means decisions about your care are made in this office, by people who see you as a neighbor, not a chart number.

Families from Crete and across southern Will County come to us for the whole span of everyday dentistry — routine cleanings and exams, fillings and crowns when life happens, and cosmetic touches when you want your smile to feel like yours again.

Because we stay small, appointments don't feel like an assembly line. You get time to ask questions, understand your options, and never feel rushed toward a treatment you don't need.

What "neighborhood practice" means here

  • One convenient location right on E Steger Rd — easy for families already living and working nearby.
  • Care for every age at one address — from a child's first cleaning to grandparents' crowns.
  • An established local reputation, earned one visit at a time — not bought with billboards.
  • Straight answers about your treatment before anything happens in the chair.
Care We Offer

Everyday dentistry, done well.

01

Exams & Cleanings — All Ages

The foundation of everything: gentle, thorough checkups and cleanings for kids, parents, and grandparents alike. Bring the whole family to one office and knock it out in one trip.

02

General & Preventive Dentistry

Catching small problems before they become big ones — screenings, preventive treatments, and honest guidance on keeping your teeth healthy between visits.

03

Restorative Care

Fillings, crowns, and bridges that restore function and comfort. When a tooth needs help, we explain what's going on in plain language and fix it right.

04

Cosmetic Dentistry & Whitening

Professional teeth whitening and cosmetic care for smiles that feel a little brighter — natural-looking results, never an overdone "TV smile."

05

Emergency Dental Visits

A cracked tooth on a Saturday morning game or a toothache that won't quit — call us first. We do our best to see local emergencies promptly.
